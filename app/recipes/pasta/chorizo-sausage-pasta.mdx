---
title: Chorizo & sausage pasta
---

# Prepare

1. Dice <Ing name="Onion" quantity={1} />.
1. Mince <Ing name="Garlic" quantity={2} unit="clove" />.
1. Slice <Ing name="Chorizo" quantity={75} unit="g" />.
1. Slice <Ing name="Silesian sausage" quantity={100} unit="g" /> into 1cm thick rounds.
1. Finely chop <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={3} />.
1. Using <Ing name="Fresh basil" quantity={1} unit="pack" />, reserve 8 leaves, and chop the rest finely (including the stalks).
1. Boil <Ing name="Pasta" quantity={200} unit="g" /> in salted water until al dente. Reserve 100ml of the starchy pasta water before draining.

# Cook

1. **Sear the sausages:** In a large pan over medium-high heat, fry the chorizo and Silesian sausage rounds for 4–5 minutes until the chorizo is crispy and the sausage edges are deeply golden and curl up. Transfer with a slotted spoon, leaving the red, smoky fat in the pan.
1. **Flavour base:** Turn the heat down to medium. Add the onion, garlic, and chilli to the remaining oil and sauté for 3 minutes until soft. Stir in <Ing name="Smoked paprika" quantity={0.5} unit="tsp" /> and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
1. **Simmer:** Pour in <Ing name="Passata" quantity={200} unit="g" /> and stir in <Ing name="MSG" quantity={0.25} unit="tsp" />. Return the meats to the pan, bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and bubble for 8–10 minutes to infuse the smoky flavours.
1. **Season:** Stir in the chopped basil.
1. **Cream:** Turn the heat to low. Pour in <Ing name="Double cream" quantity={75} unit="ml" /> and stir continuously for 1 minute until a rich, orange-pink sauce forms.
1. **Combine:** Toss the cooked pasta into the sauce with a splash of the reserved pasta water to emulsify. Serve garnished with the reserved basil leaves.
