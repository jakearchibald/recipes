---
title: Tom Yum soup
---

1. Chop & saute
   - <Ing name="Lemongrass" quantity={2} unit="stalk" />, tough outer bits
     removed, roughly chopped
   - <Ing name="Garlic" quantity={3} unit="clove" />, crushed
   - <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={4} />
   - <Ing name="Kaffir lime leaves" quantity={6} />, as is
2. Cook on high with 500ml <Ing name="Chicken stock" quantity={2} unit="pot" /> & <Ing name="Galangal paste" quantity={90} unit="g"/> for 15 minutes then blend
3. Cook for 15 mins, pulse occasionally?
   - <Ing name="Coriander" quantity={4} unit="sprig" /> stalks and smaller
     leaves
   - <Ing name="Grape tomatoes" quantity={100} unit="g" />, halved
   - <Ing name="Fish sauce" quantity={2} unit="tbsp" />
   - <Ing name="Light brown sugar" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
   - <Ing name="Mushroom" quantity={200} unit="g" />
   - <Ing name="Chicken" quantity={300} unit="g" />
   - Top up to line
4. Season with <Ing name="Lime juice" quantity={1} />, rest of coriander, maybe pulse
