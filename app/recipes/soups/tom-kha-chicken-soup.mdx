title: Tom Kha soup
---

1. Chop & saute
   1. <Ing name="Lemongrass" quantity={1} unit="stalk" />, tough outer bits
      removed, roughly chopped
   2. <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={4} />
   3. <Ing name="Kaffir lime leaves" quantity={4} />, stems removed
   4. <Ing name="Onion" quantity={1} />
2. Add and cook on high for 15 minutes then blend:
   1. 500ml <Ing name="Chicken stock" quantity={2} unit="pot" />
   2. <Ing name="Galangal paste" quantity={90} unit="g" />
   3. <Ing name="Coconut milk" quantity={350} unit="ml" />
3. Cook for 15 mins, pulse occasionally?
   1. <Ing name="Coriander" quantity={4} unit="sprig" />, chopped
   2. <Ing name="Chicken" quantity={300} unit="g" />
   3. <Ing name="Grape tomatoes" quantity={100} unit="g" />, halved
   4. <Ing name="Mushroom" quantity={200} unit="g" />
   5. Top up to line
4. Add final seasoning:
   1. <Ing name="Fish sauce" quantity={2} unit="tbsp" />
   2. <Ing name="Light brown sugar" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
   3. <Ing name="Lime juice" quantity={2} unit="tbsp" />
