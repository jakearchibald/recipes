   - <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={3} />, chopped
   - <Ing name="MSG" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />
   - <Ing name="Garlic" quantity={5} unit="clove" />, minced
1. Add to soup maker:
   - <Ing name="Chicken" quantity={500} unit="g" /> breast
   - <Ing name="Vegetable stock" quantity={2} unit="pot" /> of stock
   - <Ing name="Lime juice" quantity={1} />

1. Cook on 'smooth'.
