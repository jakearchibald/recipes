---
title: Spice bag fries
---

# Prepare

1. Spice mix, in a small bowl. Makes enough to coat the fries generously.
   - <Ing name="Chinese five-spice powder" quantity={1.5} unit="tsp"/>
   - <Ing name="Chilli powder" quantity={1.5} unit="tsp"/>
   - <Ing name="Garlic powder" quantity={1} unit="tsp"/>
   - <Ing name="Onion powder" quantity={0.5} unit="tsp"/>
   - <Ing name="Sugar" quantity={1} unit="tsp"/>
   - <Ing name="White pepper" quantity={0.5} unit="tsp"/> — gives that distinct takeaway heat
   - <Ing name="MSG" quantity={0.5} unit="tsp"/> — optional, but recommended for the umami
   - 1.5 tsp fine sea salt
1. Slice <Ing name="White onion" quantity={1} /> into half-moons.
1. Thinly slice <Ing name="Red pepper" quantity={1} /> and <Ing name="Green pepper" quantity={1} />.
1. Slice <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={2} /> into rings.

# Cook

1. Bake or air fry <Ing name="Curly fries" quantity={1} unit="bag"/> according to the package directions until deeply golden. Shake them halfway through for an even crunch.
1. Heat a wok or large frying pan over high heat until it's slightly smoking. Add a tablespoon or two of neutral oil, then immediately add the onion and peppers.
1. Stir fry for 2 minutes, until the vegetables soften slightly but keep some bite. Add the chillies for the final 30 seconds.
1. Tip the piping hot fries straight into the wok. Sprinkle the spice mix over the top to taste, and toss continuously over high heat for about a minute so it coats everything evenly.
