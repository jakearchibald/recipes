---
title: Hoisin & ginger chicken fried rice
---

Serves 2.

A weeknight fried rice — the hoisin does double duty as a marinade for the
chicken and the base of the sauce. Everything is in and out of the pan in about
ten minutes, so it's worth having all of Prepare done before you light the hob.

**Cold rice matters.** Leftover rice from the fridge, or a microwave pouch left
to cool, has dried out enough that the grains stay separate. Fresh warm rice
steams in the pan and goes gluey.

# Prepare

- Bowl — the chicken, marinating:
  - Cut <Ing name="Chicken" quantity={250} unit="g" /> (breast or skinless
    thighs) into 1cm cubes, then stir through
    <Ing name="Hoisin sauce" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" /> and
    <Ing name="Soy sauce" quantity={1} unit="tsp" /> until evenly coated.
    Leave it on the counter while you do the rest.
- Jug — the sauce, stirred together:
  - <Ing name="Soy sauce" quantity={2} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Hoisin sauce" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Water" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" /> — just to loosen the hoisin so
    it pours rather than sitting in one lump on the rice.
- Bowl — the rice:
  - <Ing name="Cooked rice" quantity={500} unit="g" />, broken up with your
    fingers so there are no clumps left. Much easier cold and out of the pan
    than hot and in it.
  - <Ing name="Garden peas" quantity={75} unit="g" /> — straight from frozen is
    fine, they thaw in seconds in the pan.
- Bowl — the onion:
  - Finely dice <Ing name="Onion" quantity={1} /> (a medium one).
- Small bowl — the aromatics:
  - <Ing name="Garlic paste" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />,
    <Ing name="Ginger paste" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />, and
    <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={1} /> finely sliced — seeds in for more heat.
- Small bowl — the eggs:
  - Beat <Ing name="Eggs" quantity={2} /> with a pinch of salt.
- Small bowl — to finish:
  - Chop <Ing name="Spring onion" quantity={2} />.

# Cook

1. **Scramble the eggs:** Heat half a tablespoon of neutral oil in a large wok
   or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour in the eggs, swirl the pan, and
   fold gently for about a minute until just set but still soft. Tip onto a
   plate — they'll finish cooking when they go back in, so under is better than
   over.
1. **Sear the chicken:** Turn the heat to high and add another half tablespoon
   of oil. Add the chicken in a single layer and leave it untouched for 2
   minutes to caramelise, then toss and fry for 2 minutes more until just cooked
   through. Add it to the plate with the eggs. There's sugar in the hoisin so it
   will catch and darken — that's the flavour you want, but don't wander off.
1. **Soften the onion:** Turn down to medium-high, add a splash more oil if the
   pan looks dry, and fry the onion for 3 minutes until softened and starting to
   colour at the edges.
1. **Aromatics:** Add the garlic, ginger and chilli and stir fry for 30 seconds,
   until fragrant. The pastes burn quickly at this heat, so keep them moving.
1. **Flash-fry the rice:** Turn the heat back to high. Tip in the rice and peas
   and toss for 2 minutes, pressing the rice against the hot pan between tosses,
   until it's steaming hot. Pour the sauce round the edge of the pan and toss for
   another minute, until every grain is evenly coloured.
1. **Finish:** Return the chicken and eggs, breaking the egg into smaller pieces
   with your spoon as you fold it through. Toss once more, then take it off the
   heat and stir in the spring onions. Serve straight away, with a pinch of
   <Ing name="Sesame seeds" /> over the top.
