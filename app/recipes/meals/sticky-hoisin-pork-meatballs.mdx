---
title: Sticky hoisin pork meatballs
---

Serves 4. From _Midweek Meals in Minutes_. Air fryer.

Meatballs in a hoisin glaze rather than a tomato sauce — quick enough for a
weeknight, and good with either rice or noodles.

# Prepare

- Bowl — the meatballs:
  - <Ing name="Pork mince" quantity={500} unit="g" /> (lean)
  - <Ing name="Panko breadcrumbs" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Eggs" quantity={1} />
  - <Ing name="Ginger paste" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />
  - <Ing name="Garlic paste" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />

  Mix together with your hands and roll into meatballs — about 20, a good bite
  each. They cook through fast, so keep them even rather than large.

- Jug — the sauce, whisked together:
  - <Ing name="Hoisin sauce" quantity={3} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Honey" quantity={2} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Smooth orange juice" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Rice vinegar" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Sriracha" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
  - <Ing name="Ground ginger" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />
  - <Ing name="Dark soy sauce" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" />
- Small bowl — to finish:
  - Julienne <Ing name="Cucumber" quantity={1} /> and <Ing name="Carrot" quantity={1} />.
  - Finely chop <Ing name="Green chillies" quantity={1} />.

# Cook

1. **Start the rice:** <Ing name="Jasmine rice" quantity={4} unit="serving" /> takes
   about 10 minutes on the hob plus 10 standing, so it can look after itself
   while the meatballs are in the fryer.
1. **Air fry the meatballs:** Remove the tray from the air fryer basket, add the
   meatballs and spray with oil. Air fry for 8–10 minutes at 200°C.
1. **Glaze:** Drizzle the sauce over the meatballs, stirring to coat. Cook for a
   further 3–4 minutes, until the sauce is reduced and sticky. Watch it towards
   the end — there's honey in there and it'll catch if you leave it.
1. **Serve:** Plate up with the rice and the cucumber and carrot, then scatter
   over the chilli and a pinch of <Ing name="Sesame seeds" /> (black and white,
   if you have both).

**Noodles instead:** cooked egg noodles work just as well — 150–200g of the
fresh kind per person. Toss them through at the glaze step so they pick up the
sauce, and increase the sauce by half, since the noodles drink it up.
