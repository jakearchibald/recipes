---
title: Sriracha chicken stir fry
---

Serves 2.

# Prepare

- Bowl — the chicken:
  - Cut <Ing name="Chicken" quantity={300} unit="g" /> into bite-sized pieces, then toss with <Ing name="Chinese five-spice powder" quantity={0.25} unit="tsp" />, <Ing name="Cornflour" quantity={0.5} unit="tsp" /> and a tiny pinch of salt. The cornflour is what gives it that silky takeaway texture, and helps the glaze grip.
- Jug — the sauce, whisked together:
  - <Ing name="Soy sauce" quantity={2} unit="tbsp" /> — low-sodium is best
  - <Ing name="Sriracha" quantity={1.5} unit="tbsp" /> — adjust to taste
  - <Ing name="Light brown sugar" quantity={1.5} unit="tbsp" /> (or maple syrup)
  - <Ing name="Rice vinegar" quantity={2} unit="tsp" />
  - <Ing name="MSG" quantity={0.25} unit="tsp" />
  - <Ing name="Cornflour" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />, blended with
    <Ing name="Water" quantity={1} unit="tbsp" /> of cold water in a small cup
    first, then stirred in.
- Bowl — the veg, which all goes in together:
  - <Ing name="Broccoli" quantity={100} unit="g" /> — about a third of a typical UK head, which is 300–400g with the stalk on:
    - Sit the head stalk-down and slice the florets away where they meet the thick central stalk, then halve or quarter any that are bigger than one bite.
    - Peel the woody skin off the stalk and slice the pale core into thin coins. It stir-fries like the florets, so count it towards the 100g.
  - Thinly slice <Ing name="Red pepper" quantity={1} /> and <Ing name="Carrot" quantity={1} /> (a small one).
  - Finely slice <Ing name="Chillies" quantity={2} /> — keep the seeds for maximum heat, or use one for something gentler.
  - <Ing name="Garden peas" quantity={75} unit="g" />, thawed if they're frozen.
- Small bowl — the aromatics:
  - Mince <Ing name="Garlic" quantity={2} unit="clove" /> and grate <Ing name="Fresh ginger" quantity={1} unit="tsp" />.
- Small bowl — to finish:
  - Chop <Ing name="Spring onion" quantity={2} />.

# Cook

1. **Sear the chicken:** Heat a tablespoon of neutral oil in a large wok or frying pan over high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and sear untouched for 2 minutes, then toss and fry for 1 minute more, until almost cooked through. Transfer to a plate — it finishes in the glaze, so leaving it just under is what keeps it juicy.
1. **Flash-fry the veg:** Add another half tablespoon of oil to the hot pan, then tip in the bowl of veg. Stir fry over high heat for 3 minutes, until the broccoli is bright green and tender-crisp.
1. **Aromatics:** Push the veg to the edge of the pan. Drop the garlic and ginger into the centre and stir fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
1. **Glaze and thicken:** Turn the heat down to medium and return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Give the sauce a quick stir, pour it over everything, and toss constantly for 1 minute until it bubbles into a thick, glossy glaze.
1. **Finish:** Off the heat, stir in the spring onions. Serve immediately, garnished with toasted <Ing name="Sesame seeds" /> and a squeeze of <Ing name="Lime juice" quantity={1} />.

**Rice:** start it before the Cook steps — jasmine rice wants about 10 minutes
on the hob plus 10 standing, so it can look after itself while you're at the
wok. Two servings for this amount.

**Fresh noodles:** the refrigerated or vacuum-packed kind, 150–200g per
person.
Separate them under warm water and drain well, then add them at the glaze step,
tipping them in right after the sauce and tossing for a minute or two until
they're coated and hot through. Increase the sauce by half — noodles drink it
up. Don't put them in any earlier or they break up and go gluey, and if the pan
looks crowded, glaze in two batches rather than fighting it.
